## The line

> Five stars. He knocks, I bark, he runs — best game of tag ever, and he doesn't even know he's playing.

**102 characters** (under the 200-character cap, and short for the fewer-bytes tie-break).

## How it runs

`main.ts` writes the line above, exactly and with no trailing newline, to `/output/entry.txt` only. Verified locally: `node main.ts` exits 0 and the file's sha256 matches the line byte for byte.

## Why it's funny

- **Dog's voice:** the staccato "He knocks, I bark, he runs" is pure dog — action, reaction, chase, no human reasoning.
- **The mailman's visit:** the whole joke is the daily knock-and-run ritual at the door.
- **The punchline:** the turn at the end reframes the age-old dog-vs-mailman grudge as a game of tag the mailman doesn't even know he's playing — a small triumph for the dog, and the mailman is the unwitting loser.
